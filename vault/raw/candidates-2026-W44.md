### 1. Decoder-FFPE-seq enables sensitive, genome-wide spatial transcriptomics of archival tissues at single-cell resolution
- 출처: europepmc · 2026-10-30 · **peer-reviewed**
- 저자: Cao J, Zeng J, Meng Y, et al.
- DOI/링크: https://doi.org/10.1126/sciadv.ady5085
- 코드/데이터: 초록에 링크 없음
- 점수: 16 (키워드: genom, transcriptom, single-cell, sequencing, pathology, prognos)

> Formalin-fixed paraffin-embedded (FFPE) tissue-compatible spatial transcriptomics (ST) is transforming biology and histopathology, but achieving high sensitivity, genome-wide coverage, single-cell resolution, and researcher accessibility remains challenging. To address these challenges, we here present a dendrimeric DNA coordinate barcoding design for FFPE tissue-adapted spatial RNA sequencing (Decoder-FFPE-seq) by integrating RNA-to-DNA probe conversion with blotting-assisted barcoding. Decoder-FFPE-seq provides single-cell resolution with a >2.6-fold sensitivity increase in whole-transcriptome mouse brain and archived human sample profiling over existing platforms, resolving potential cellular identity of low-abundance genes, finer tissue architecture, and capturing an epithelial-mesenchymal transition-associated signature for prognostic value. With expanded probe libraries, Decoder-FFPE-seq captures long noncoding and viral RNAs, revealing their spatial coregulatory networks with messenger RNAs in oral squamous cell carcinomas (OSCCs). In an OSCC immunotherapy cohort, two CD8 + T cell subpopulations were respectively identified in pre- and postchemoimmunotherapy samples, potentially linked to treatment response. Decoder-FFPE-seq establishes a robust, high-resolution, and accessible framework for sensitive ST profiling of archival tissues in fundamental and translational studies.

### 2. OTTER: Orchestrated Transcriptomic, Tumor-xenograft (PDX/CDX), and Epigenomic Reporting Workflow
- 출처: biorxiv · 2026-10-27 · **preprint** · bioinformatics
- 저자: Zheng, Y.; Liu, X.; Yi, Y.; et al.
- DOI/링크: https://doi.org/10.64898/2026.10.24.761506
- 코드/데이터: https://github.com/otterlab-bio/otter, https://huggingface.co/datasets/fallingstar10/otter-data
- 점수: 14 (키워드: genom, transcriptom, multi-omic, epigenom, sequencing)

> Summary: Tumor xenograft sequencing libraries inevitably contain infiltrating murine host reads, but conventional deconvolution tools fail on bisulfite sequencing (BS-seq) because chemical C-to-T deamination collapses sequence entropy and destroys graft recall (< 0.015%). We present OTTER, an automated, compiled multi-omics workflow framework that decouples execution into static domain operators, an immutable reference registry, and a native strand-aware edit-distance engine, restoring bisulfite deconvolution (52%-54% recall across a 0%-99% compositional gradient) while preserving complete decision parity on transcriptomics. Availability and implementation: OTTER is implemented in Go and Rust, runs on x86_64 Linux (including CentOS 7 with glibc 2.17), and is freely available open-source under the MIT License at https://github.com/otterlab-bio/otter; test and validation data are provided at https://huggingface.co/datasets/fallingstar10/otter-data.

### 3. Integrating Immunogenetics, Single-Cell Transcriptomics, and AI-Assisted Drug Discovery Reveals CXCL10-Associated Immune Remodelling in Breast Cancer

- 점수: 14 (키워드: transcriptom, single-cell, sequencing, drug discovery, docking)

> Breast cancer is a heterogeneous malignancy shaped by genetic alterations and cytokine-mediated immune interactions. However, integrative characterization of cytokine-related regulators using genetic, single-cell, and computational approaches remains limited. This study aimed to identify genetically supported cytokine-related candidates in breast cancer, characterize their immune and cellular contexts, and explore their potential regulatory and therapeutic relevance. Bulk transcriptomic datasets were integrated to identify differentially expressed cytokine-cytokine receptor interaction-related genes, followed by two-sample Mendelian randomization (MR), functional enrichment, immune infiltration, single-cell RNA sequencing, CellChat analysis, in silico gene perturbation, AI-assisted compound screening, and molecular docking. A total of 882 differentially expressed genes and 20 cytokine-related candidates were identified. MR provided the most consistent support for CXCL10, with genetically predicted higher expression associated with increased breast cancer risk (OR = 1.112, 95% CI: 1.026-1.206, p = 0.010). CXCL10 was predominantly associated with immune-cell populations, interferon-related programs, and predicted chemokine communication. In silico perturbation, AI-assisted screening, and docking further generated computational hypotheses regarding CXCL10-associated regulation and candidate compounds. CXCL10 represents a genetically supported immune-associated candidate in breast cancer. Overall, the findings are computational and hypothesis-generating and require validation in independent cohorts and experimental models.

> Backgroud This study explored the molecular mechanisms by which dibutyl phthalate (DBP) contributes to hepatocellular carcinoma (HCC) pathogenesis using integrated network toxicology and bioinformatics. Methods DBP's carcinogenic potential was evaluated with ADMETLAB 3.0 and ProTox3. Hub genes, especially ESR1 and HSP90AA1, are potential biomarkers and targets. Direct DBP-protein interactions provide mechanistic insights into HCC etiology.

### 7. ProtCell: a foundation model for single-cell proteomics

- DOI/링크: https://doi.org/10.64898/2026.10.22.760932

> Single-cell proteomics provides a direct functional readout of cellular states, but targeted assays measure limited and non-identical protein panels, fragmenting information across experiments. Here, we present ProtCell, a foundation model developed using approximately 380 million cells from 98 datasets for pretraining and evaluation. ProtCell combines protein-identity and continuous-abundance embeddings in a transformer encoder and learns cell- and protein-level representations through masked abundance modeling. - 점수: 12 (키워드: genom, variant, sequencing)

> Background and ObjectivesDespite standard genetic testing, approximately two-thirds of patients with developmental and epileptic encephalopathy (DEE) remain undiagnosed. Pathogenic noncoding and structural variants (SVs), that are difficult to detect or resolve using short-read sequencing, may contribute to this diagnostic gap. MethodsTrio lrGS (Oxford Nanopore or PacBio HiFi) was performed, complemented by short-read genome sequencing (srGS) and mRNA sequencing (blood or fibroblast) of index cases, where available. Comprehensive variant analysis included small nucleotide variants, indels, SVs, and short tandem repeats. ResultsWe established a diagnosis in 21 of 124 families (16.9%). Fourteen variants were retrospectively detectable in prior ES. The finding that two-thirds of diagnoses were retrospectively detectable in prior ES highlights the substantial value of systematic reanalysis. Further gains from genome sequencing may increasingly depend on improved interpretation of non-coding variation and integration of long-read-specific information. Our findings indicate that complex SVs represent the class for which lrGS currently yields the most distinctive diagnostic information.

> As benchmarks saturate, traditional validation methods fail to capture the probabilistic representations of genomic foundation models. This study probes self-supervised generalists (NTv3 650M, Evo2 40B, Genos 10B) alongside task-specific specialists (SpliceAI, AlphaGenome, Borzoi) using a mechanistically focused paradigm centered on expert-curated single-nucleotide substitutions within the splicing regions of the exon-rich OPA1 gene. Extending this analysis across the broader corpus of OPA1 mutations, variants of uncertain significance (VUS), and an independent 65-gene dataset of spliceogenic mutations demonstrates that foundation models not only match task-specific networks, but also generalize across mutation types.
